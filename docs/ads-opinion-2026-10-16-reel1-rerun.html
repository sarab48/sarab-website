SARAB · Meta ads · opinion · 16 October 2026

Should the 10.2026 campaign be an almost-exact rerun of reel1?

Numbers pulled today from the ad account and the bookings database. Nothing in the account was changed.

Yes, run it. Reel1 in the North is still the best booking engine the account has ever had, and the Central test confirmed the opposite lesson: reel1 only works where SARAB is already known.

But an almost-identical rerun is a safe restart, not a growth move. The North audience is rested, not new. Expect it to start cheaper than it ended in August and to tire faster than it did in June. Two changes raise the ceiling without extra budget: put a second reel beside reel1 in the same ad set instead of running it alone, and let the caption carry the one piece of news the audience does not have yet: 2027 dates are being booked now.

Why the North stopped in August

  • The audience was used up, not the video. 72,741 people reached, 3.8 views each. Weekly cost per conversation went ₪3.6 at launch, ₪15 to ₪25 through June and July, then ₪33, ₪41 and ₪55 in the three August weeks.
  • People who messaged still booked at the same rate (7% lifetime, 14% in the WhatsApp era). Fewer people messaged. That is the signature of fatigue.
  • Seven weeks off and a few new towns bring part of that back, not all of it. Meta still serves the ad first to the same high-intent people, and most of them have already seen it four times.

What Central actually proved

  • The region was expensive (CPM ₪33 vs ₪28, click rate half the North's), and the two Ads Manager saves on Aug 31 restarted learning mid-run.
  • Reel1 was the weak ad there, the WOW reel carried it. In the final week reel1-CT cost ₪130 per conversation; WOW-CT cost ₪23.5 and owned the only booking.
  • So going back to the North with reel1 is the correct reading. The same logic says a cold town inside the new city list will not respond to reel1 the way Nazareth did.

What I would change in the plan

1. Don't replace reel1. Put a second reel next to it.

Budget sits on the ad set, so a second ad does not halve reel1's reach; Meta sends 80 to 90% of spend to whichever ad converts cheaper. A losing second ad costs a few shekels a day. A winning one covers the week-four fade that will come sooner this time.

  1. Best: a new reel built on reel1's formula with new faces. A group of friends at a bride's night, the pose, the print landing in a hand, end on the after-portrait. October has 14 events on the calendar, so the footage exists. New faces reset the "seen this" reflex while keeping the structure that produced 35 bookings.
  2. If no new reel can be ready: the WOW reel. Rested since Aug 25, it carried Central and closed better than reel1 in the North (10.7% vs 7.1% of conversations booked). It is a weaker lead buyer than reel1 at home, which is exactly why it belongs as the backup ad, not the only one.

Running the WOW reel instead of reel1 would be a step back (₪322 per booking vs ₪228, and it burned out in five weeks). Running a brand-new reel alone is a gamble. Both reels in one ad set is the free A/B.

2. The caption is the smallest lever. Change it only to add news.

  • About two thirds of delivery was Instagram Stories and Reels, where the caption is barely read. The first two seconds of video do the selling.
  • A new caption creates a new post, so the visible counters restart (247 reactions, 106 saves, 2 comments on the June post). Small loss, acceptable.
  • The only new fact worth adding is the season. A third of all bookings are made six or more months ahead, and 22 events already sit on the 2027 calendar. October buyers are planning spring and summer 2027. Say so.

3. Before publishing (the API can see the draft campaign, not its ad sets)

  • The duplicate copied the whole campaign: the reel1 ad set, the WOW ad set and the old "Copy" test. Only one ad set should be on.
  • The draft shows a campaign-level budget setting. The original ran ₪100/day on the ad set. Keep ₪100/day on one ad set, not ₪150 (that burned the WOW ad set in five weeks).
  • Re-set the WhatsApp welcome and pre-filled message on the new ad and attach the Instagram identity. A copy does not guarantee either.
  • Map the new ad IDs in the office WhatsApp tab and add a campaign source before the first lead arrives, or every booking falls into the generic Meta source.
  • After it goes live, do not edit the ad "to fix a detail". Every save re-mints the post and restarts learning. Batch fixes into the next creative swap.

What to expect, and when to act

My estimate for the rerun is ₪20 to ₪35 per conversation in the first weeks, drifting upward. The bar is simple: it must beat Central's ₪37.5, or the audience has not recovered. At ₪30 and the North's close rates, a booking costs ₪215 to ₪420 against an average ticket of about ₪1,850. Profitable. The open question is lifespan; four to six good weeks is realistic, not eleven.

Rule: if the 7-day cost per conversation stays above ₪45 for two weeks in a row, or there is no deposit by day 21, add a new reel as a new ad, then pause the loser. Never edit the existing ad.

New reel: the on-video hook (added 19 October)

The new wedding montage has no on-screen text. Put one short line on frames 0 to 2.5 s, top third, below the top 10% of the frame (the Reels interface covers that strip), white bold with a soft shadow, the same style as the WOW hook. Off before the tablet reveal at about 2 s so the reveal stays clean. Never over a face. Three to five words. Caption A then answers the hook with the one piece of news: 2027 dates are booking.
